Who We Are

We are a dedicated group of innovators pushing boundaries in the FIRST Tech Challenge track. As one of only three operational FTC teams in Manitoba, we don't just build competitive robots we actively engineer pathways to guide, mentor, and bootstrap incoming rookie teams across the province.

Our Mission

"To provide innovative, hands-on, and accessible STEM opportunities to students within Manitoba."

Manitoba deserves more tech extracurriculars. Centurion Robotics exists to close that gap, giving public school students real access to engineering, structural electrical designs, and advanced Java logic loops before they ever set foot in a university auditorium.

What is FIRST Tech Challenge?

It's way more than building robots. FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) tasks teams of up to 15 students with designing, prototyping, programming, and operating heavy alliance robots to solve complex field challenges. Guided by technical mentors, students manage budgets, build brand recognition, market to sponsors, and run community outreach programs.

Why Join the Track?

Qualify for over $80 Million in STEM college scholarships.
Prototype with industrial-grade mechanical components and sensors.
Write real-world complex object-oriented software scripts in Java.
2.1x more likely to declare engineering and computer science majors as alumni.
