Fargo, North Dakota Scrimmage

Apr. 6th, 2024

We were invited to Fargo, ND for a friendly competition! It was a great learning experience for all of us. Getting to talk to experienced teams about robot design strategies, talking to mentors about building better team organization, and meeting many like-minded students, overall it was an awesome experience.

A sincere thanks to FTC Dakota for inviting us to the event, we look forward to even more competitions in the future!

Manitoba Schools Science Symposium

Apr. 22nd, 2023

We did an outreach program at the Manitoba Schools Science Symposium! We had the opportunity to talk to many students, teachers and parents about robotic programs in Manitoba. We also demoed the robot to people present at the fair..

Thank you to the MSSS for having us!

Manitoba First Nations Schools Science Fair

March 8, 2023

We did an outreach program at the Manitoba First Nations Schools Science Fair! We had the opportunity to talk to many students and teachers about robotic programs in Manitoba. We also had a chance to present to Wab Kinew, the leader of the Manitoba NDP.

Thank you to the MFNERC for having us!
